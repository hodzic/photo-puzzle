Use the address of the picture itself. In Google Photos, open the photo, then right-click it (or long-press on a phone) and choose Copy image address. A shared album's link (photos.app.goo.gl) is a page, not a picture, so it won't work here.

Tap a tile in the gap's row or column to slide it, along with any tiles between. Arrow keys work too. To share a photo puzzle, use a photo from Photo link; photos from this device stay on it.
